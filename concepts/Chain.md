# Chain

A chain is a run of consecutive removal steps triggered by a single piece lock. The step counter starts at 1 and multiplies the points of reactions and line clears at each step, so deeper chains pay disproportionately. The status line shows "cadena ×N" from the second step on.

## Structural
- enables → [[Cascade trivializes difficulty]] because a lucky chain can empty most of the board
