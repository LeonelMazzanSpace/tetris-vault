# Pure line

A completed row whose ten cells all share one element (`isPure`). It earns a flat 500 points times the level on top of the normal line score, and the status line shows "¡Línea pura!". Because elements are assigned per piece at random, it rewards planning around the current piece colours rather than luck alone.
