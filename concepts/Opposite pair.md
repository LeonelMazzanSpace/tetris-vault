# Opposite pair

Elements are paired as opposites: fire with water, earth with air (the `OPPOSITE` table). Two cells touching orthogonally with opposite elements are the trigger condition for a reaction. Pairs are fixed and symmetric, so the player can always predict what a placement will touch.
